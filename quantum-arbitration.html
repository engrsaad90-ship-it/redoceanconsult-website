Quantum & dispute avoidance

Put a figure on delay and protect your position

Rates are built from your own records, with a cost summary an engineer can examine line by line. Each cost head ties to a proven delay event, so entitlement and amount tell one story.

  1. Identify cost heads and the entitlement basis
  2. Collect and verify records: timesheets, plant, invoices, payments
  3. Build rates and calculate each head with its time link
  4. Check for double counting and apply any contractual limits
  5. Present an auditable claim schedule

Arbitration & expert services

One consistent story from schedule to hearing

When a dispute reaches arbitration, technical evidence must stay clear under scrutiny. We keep technical issues apart from legal argument, with every finding traceable to records.

  1. Early case assessment: test each position on time and cost
  2. Evidence build: chronology linked to programme and cost records
  3. Expert reports: independent time and quantum reports
  4. Responding to the other side: rebuttals of method and calculation gaps
  5. Witness and hearing support: witness prep and hearing presentations
  6. Post-hearing support: closing submissions and technical clarifications
