Who we are

Hands-on construction experience, applied to programmes and claims

Red Ocean Consult was founded in 2019, born out of years spent on live construction sites across Saudi Arabia. Time and again, our founders watched capable contractors carry the cost of delays they had not caused. Late access, delayed approvals, design changes and late instructions from the employer pushed completion dates back, yet the contractor was left to absorb the time, the prolongation costs and, too often, liquidated damages.

The problem was rarely a lack of entitlement. It was a lack of evidence. Programmes were not kept up to date, records were scattered, and delay events were never linked to the critical path in a way an engineer or tribunal could follow.

We set up Red Ocean Consult to change that. We combined hands-on construction experience with forensic scheduling, so that every delay is traced to its cause, every day of extension is supported by records, and every claim can be followed from event to entitlement.

Forensic planning

Delay analysis & extension of time. Prove the cause, recover the time.

The method follows the evidence. Excusable and compensable delay are assessed, concurrency is tested and each event is linked to the critical path.

Our approach

  1. 01

    Gather the record

    Contract, baseline, all updates, notices, instructions, correspondence and site records.

  2. 02

    Check programme integrity

    Logic, constraints, calendars and critical path before any analysis is relied on.

  3. 03

    Build the event register

    Every delay event with its date, cause, responsible party and contractual basis.

  4. 04

    Select the method

    The method follows the available records and the question to be answered.

  5. 05

    Analyse and test

    Quantify each event's effect on completion; test concurrency and sensitivity.

  6. 06

    Report

    A forensic report with transparent assumptions and supporting records.

Methods we use

As-planned vs as-built

Compares the baseline with actual progress to show where slippage arose. Suited to simpler projects or limited records.

Impacted as-planned

Inserts delay events into the baseline to model their effect. Useful when updates are not available, with its limits stated.

Time impact analysis

Inserts each event into the programme as it stood at the time, update by update, to measure its effect on the critical path.

Windows analysis

Divides the project into time windows and examines how the critical path shifted in each. Well suited to projects with regular updates.

Quantum & dispute avoidance

Put a figure on delay and protect your position

Rates are built from your own records, with a cost summary an engineer can examine line by line. Each cost head ties to a proven delay event, so entitlement and amount tell one story.

  1. Identify cost heads and the entitlement basis
  2. Collect and verify records: timesheets, plant, invoices, payments
  3. Build rates and calculate each head with its time link
  4. Check for double counting and apply any contractual limits
  5. Present an auditable claim schedule
