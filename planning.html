Planning & baselines

An approved baseline. Honest progress reporting.

Primavera P6 baselines built on contract review and approved by the consultant, then updated every period so slippage is caught early.

Baseline programme

Build an approvable baseline

A baseline is the reference every delay is measured against. We build yours in Primavera P6 from the contract up, and keep at it until the consultant approves it.

  1. Contract and scope review: milestones, constraints and completion obligations
  2. WBS and activity logic in Primavera P6, with calendars and critical path checks
  3. Resource loading: cost, manpower and BOQ mapping to activities
  4. Duration testing and a schedule narrative explaining the plan
  5. Consultant comments incorporated until approval, with every revision traceable
Progress reporting

Report the position honestly

Periodic updates explain variance, float movement and critical path changes, so you get early warning while recovery is still possible, and records that can later substantiate a claim.

  1. Monthly programme updates with variance narratives
  2. S-curves, SPI and CPI every period
  3. Excel and Power BI dashboards for executives and site teams
  4. Milestone tracking and look-aheads for site coordination
